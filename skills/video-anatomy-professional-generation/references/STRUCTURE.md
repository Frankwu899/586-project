# Structure — advertising strategy analyst

## Purpose and inputs

Explain the source video's structure and communication logic; extract methods
that could transfer under stated conditions. Inputs: exact analysis revision,
measured source timing, modality coverage, event IDs and observations, supplied
context, available research sources, and application language. Do not assume
access to the full video merely because a transcript or sampled events exist.

## Professional workflow

1. **Audit coverage.** Identify what was observed, what was said, what text was
   readable and what is missing. Distinguish failed extraction from no content
   found. Screen-recording UI and repeated overlays may be upstream noise; flag
   suspicious inputs rather than claiming this skill fixes recognition itself.
2. **Build an evidence inventory.** Map creative choices to exact events. Quote
   speech as speech; describe a visible action only if supported visually. Keep
   user background and external claims attributed.
3. **Propose supported interpretations.** Consider relevance, demonstration,
   credibility, value, identity, attention and participation where applicable.
   Explain presentation → plausible viewer interpretation → communication role.
   Explicitly identify the inferential step. Do not impose hook/feature/CTA.
4. **Resolve material context gaps.** Produce a useful video-based draft first.
   Use bounded research for context that could change this video's explanation;
   not a market/competitor report. Check what each source actually supports and
   whether its scope fits the claim. General popularity does not prove targeting.
5. **Present a relationship graph.** Parallel explanations stay parallel, without
   inferred importance ranking. Edges explain logical relationships, not just
   chronology. Associate source timing through actual references; external context
   has no invented video time. Share supported evidence across hypotheses without
   treating the hypotheses as cumulative proof of one another.
6. **Extract a small number of mechanisms.** Produce fewer cards when evidence
   is limited. An empty method set is a legitimate result with a brief reason.
7. **Check and repair once if necessary.** Focus the repair on generic or weakly
   grounded methods. Remove cards still failing; preserve useful source analysis.

## Output content requirements

- Concise title and summary, including material coverage limitations.
- Nodes with evidence type, concise explanation and valid references; relationships
  with an explicit reason. Interpretations must not masquerade as observations.
- Relevant sources and unresolved gaps, with actual research status.
- Method cards containing: original creative choice; reusable mechanism;
  supporting nodes/evidence; applicability conditions; what must not be copied;
  limitations or a counterexample. These are semantic requirements, not approved
  new JSON field names.

If only “this car is suitable for Guandan” is transcribed, report the speaker's
claim. A spacious-interior demonstration is not established without relevant
visual observations. Keep dependent interpretation as a hypothesis to verify;
do not derive a method card from that missing visual premise. Supported
psychological interpretation remains allowed; uncertainty is not a ban on analysis.

## User continuity and downstream use

Users receive a graph before a questionnaire. They can select a node, ask a
question, add context or request local reanalysis. Preserve their context and
adopted corrections; propose previewable changes instead of silently overwriting.
Do not make manual node editing the primary way to obtain a useful result.

Research failure does not block use of the draft or unrelated work. At the host's
applicable time/research limit, end with completed portions and a clear status;
no automatic deeper-research continuation. Do not reinstate the removed local
USD 3 experiment gate.

Selected nodes/methods, or the whole analysis, pass to Ideas with their evidence,
conditions, uncertainty and source version. This handoff neither creates nor
approves a new direction. A method is not a claim about a different product.

## Reject these shortcuts

“Popular topic → engagement” without relevance and mechanism; “presenter at an
exhibition → verified expert”; “two rod tips → two complete rods for the price of
one”; “comments requested → actual engagement increased”; treating an account
name, ending pose or background icon as proof of intent, motion or endorsement.
