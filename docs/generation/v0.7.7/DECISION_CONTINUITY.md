# Decision continuity

## New authoring direction
The owner requested a distillation of professional advertising practice across
Structure, Ideas and Storyboard, with concise, prominent, clean output; basic
contextual research; and consequential professional questions. The owner then
authorized revising the three skills. The shared workflow and editorial/research
guides implement this direction. No new score threshold, budget or model is chosen.

## Interpretation of the new editorial direction
Goal/message/audience/hook are analytical questions to resolve where supported,
not mandatory fields filled with guesses. Element functions explain consequential
choices; do not invent deliberate intent for every object. Supported alternatives
stay parallel. A concise diagnosis accompanies the graph, not a replacement for it.
Concision does not hide missing evidence, discard required shot content or flatten
the stored semantic fields.
